# UX Contract — SOP Fieldguide

## Primary job

Help an operator find and apply the right general cross-border SOP, U.S. TikTok Shop execution step, Agent prompt, template, platform reference, or example; guide a beginner through preparing one SKU for a U.S. TikTok Shop launch.

## Navigation and state

- The home route explains both the upstream seven-agent path and the dedicated U.S. TikTok Shop route. The TikTok route begins with seller eligibility, before SKU or inventory work.
- `#studio` is the TikTok SOP execution workbench: a stage rail maps to the eight U.S. workflow documents; selecting a stage opens its task checklist and relevant local calculator or draft tool.
- Stage selection is bookmarkable. A clear prior/next action and focus restoration preserve orientation when switching stages.
- The eligibility choice is operator-reported, never inferred by the app. SOP reading and planning remain available; an unresolved or negative eligibility state is visibly marked STOP and is never rendered as platform approval.
- Checklist progress, non-sensitive product brief fields, manual unit-economics inputs, and script drafts are stored in browser `localStorage` only. The workbench shows this boundary, offers JSON export/import and explicit clear/replace confirmation, handles storage failures, and pauses on a newer workspace from another tab.
- Script drafts are deterministic templates populated only with operator-entered facts. They are not generated by an AI service, not a TikTok policy check, and not approved for publishing.
- Unit economics uses per-order values entered by the operator; no platform fee or commission is assumed. It reports an estimated contribution before/after advertising, not net profit, and names excluded tax/fixed costs.
- The library route groups documents by method, Agent, shared template, example, and U.S. TikTok Shop.
- A selected document is encoded in the URL hash so it can be shared and restored.
- Search is local to the bundled repository material; it searches titles, paths, and document text.
- The document reader can copy content, download a Markdown copy, and open the canonical GitHub source.
- Each app route sets a contextual Chinese document title.

## Source and compliance

- Display the upstream repository and MIT license attribution, while linking project-authored TikTok Shop documents to the user's repository.
- Bundle the maintained Markdown, YAML, and text guidance only.
- Do not bundle or expose the unedited source PDF referenced by the upstream README.
- Date-stamp official TikTok Shop policy snapshots. Live Seller Center instructions take precedence; no policy value is represented as live data.
- Show the U.S. seller eligibility limitation before launch actions. If an operator's legal route is not confirmed, the route is a stop, not a workaround.
- The fieldguide and workbench are static/browser-local tools: they do not connect to TikTok accounts, read shop data, call an AI API, post content, or process orders. No account, tax, customer, credential, or supplier-secret data belongs in local fields or exported backups.
- Clearing a local workspace permanently removes its saved tasks and draft data from this browser. Export before clearing if a backup is wanted.

## Accessibility

- Use semantic landmarks, native buttons and links, labeled search, visible keyboard focus, and responsive navigation.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Sanitize rendered Markdown HTML before insertion into the document.
- Product forms use application-owned validation and labeled fields; browser-local saved state has an explicit local/saved/conflict/error status.
